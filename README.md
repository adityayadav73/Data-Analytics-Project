## 👩‍💼 3️⃣ Employee Data Analysis
## 🔎 Project Objective
 The objective of this project was to analyze the relationship between employee experience and salary to understand how compensation grows with experience.
🧹 Data Preparation
Cleaned missing salary values
Converted experience and salary columns into numeric format
Removed inconsistent records
## 📊 Key Insights
Salary shows a positive correlation with experience.
Employees with higher experience tend to earn higher salaries.
Some outliers indicate unusually high or low salaries.
Salary growth is not perfectly linear, suggesting other influencing factors.
## 📈 Visualizations
 A scatter plot was created to analyze the correlation between years of experience and salary.
